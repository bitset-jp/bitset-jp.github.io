**Pondashi-kun Video Ver.2** shipped in **May 2023** with **seek-from-position**, **pause/resume**, **interlaced/progressive** switching, and **XInput gamepad** control. Full command details are on **[bitset.jp’s command reference](https://bitset.jp/download_file/view/323/323)** and the **[product page](/en/products/pondashi-video)**.
