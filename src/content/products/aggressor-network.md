AGGRESSOR NETWORK is a **hardware network emulator** that reproduces the hard-to-test faults real networks inflict—delay (with jitter), loss, duplication, single-bit corruption, reordering, and bandwidth limits from bps through Gbps—so teams can raise confidence in networked products before they ship.

On the bench you get dual **1 Gbps wired** ports, a **Full HD HDMI** screen for status and setup, and a **keyboard-driven** command interface. PCAP capture stays compatible with **Wireshark** and can be saved to USB, matching the workflow on [bitset.jp](https://bitset.jp/products/aggressor-network).

A built-in **RTC (CR1225)** supports time-aware scenarios and disciplined logging. For QA and network-product teams who have outgrown software-only throttling on a single PC, AGGRESSOR NETWORK is the dedicated appliance for **repeatable impairment** between two wired paths.
