ECHOSET is an **ECHONET Lite** housing-equipment **emulator**: you can exercise and demonstrate home controllers **without** a wall of physical air conditioners, breakers, solar gear, storage, water heaters, floor heating, fuel cells, lighting, switches, and other classes bitset documents on the [legacy product page](https://bitset.jp/products/echoset).

Coverage follows **standard ECHONET Lite properties** with **manufacturer code switching**—intentionally **not** vendor-unique features—so tests stay portable across the ecosystem. bitset lists **Panasonic Home Navigation HF-MC10A2GE** among verified controllers.

At **¥35,000 (excluding tax)**, the box includes the main unit, power adapter, and manual/warranty (**one year**). Purchase through **Amazon.co.jp** or **direct contact** with bitset, matching the options published on bitset.jp.
