Pondashi-kun Audio is the **event-focused “pon dashi” player** co-developed with recording engineer **Mr. Asayama**: press a key and the cue fires—built for **live reliability**, not smartphone UX. Field trials and working sound engineers shaped the feature set described on [bitset.jp](https://bitset.jp/products/products-pondash).

A **hardware USB keypad** delivers tactile certainty; **USB memory** holds unlimited tracks (within drive limits) and can be **swapped during playback**. **MP3, WAV, and AAC** cover common formats, with **hi-res WAV** paths when you pair a capable **USB-DAC**. Choose **LCD** (countdown and status) or **BASIC** (no panel). Power from **Micro USB at 5 V / 2 A+**, including mobile batteries—bitset cites **5+ hours** on a **10,000 mAh** pack as an example, varying with your DAC chain.

Retail SKUs on **Amazon.co.jp** include **BASIC** and **LCD** variants; see the legacy page for catalogs, pricing, and contact options for non-Amazon purchases or demo loans.
