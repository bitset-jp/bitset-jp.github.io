Pondashi-kun Mini is billed on [bitset.jp](https://bitset.jp/products/products-pondash-mini) as a **FRISK-sized Full HD** player: pocketable, **auto-playing** when power is applied, looping **dictionary-ordered** files from USB, and running from **microUSB** including mobile batteries.

You prepare **USB storage** and a **USB power adapter**; the box includes the main unit plus **mini HDMI→HDMI** and **microUSB→USB** adapters. Audio rides **HDMI** (no USB-DAC path). An optional **paid repack** into a FRISK case is available—bitset handles disposal of the original packaging contents.
