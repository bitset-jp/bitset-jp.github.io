Pondashi-kun Network adds **Ethernet orchestration** on top of the **Video** feature set: a **parent** issues playback commands while **child** units (bitset documents up to **250** per deployment) render **Full HD** MP4 (**< 20 Mbps** recommended) and stills from **their own USB** sticks—so scaling out does not centralize decode load.

Wiring uses ordinary **switches and copper**; bitset highlights that you do **not** need **HDMI-over-Ethernet converters** for long reach when the network path is Ethernet-native. Nodes may be **added or removed during playback** because each player is autonomous.

Pricing is **made-to-order**; bitset publishes sample **tax-included** figures on [bitset.jp](https://bitset.jp/products/products-pondash-network) (minimum parent+child bundle and per-child add-ons). Start with their **contact form** for quotes. The architecture is **patent applied** in Japan (**2017-239266**).
