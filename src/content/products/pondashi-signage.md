Pondashi-kun Signage targets **simple digital signage**: plug **HDMI** into a **PC monitor you already own**, supply **MP4** files on **USB**, and power the player over **microUSB**. On power-up it **auto-plays** and **loops** files in **dictionary order**, with **hot-swap** support for the USB stick.

If you need **sound**, use a **monitor with speakers** or add **external speakers**—audio is carried on **HDMI**, and there is **no USB-DAC** audio path. Retail purchase is available on **Amazon.co.jp**; see [bitset.jp](https://bitset.jp/products/products-pondash-signage) for pricing sheets and contact options.
