Pondashi-kun Video is the **Full HD** sibling to the audio line: **USB-stored** MP4 (plus **JPEG/PNG** stills), **USB keypad** control, and HDMI output with audio on the same cable—no USB-DAC audio path.

**Version 2 (May 2023)** adds **seek-from-position**, **pause/resume**, **time nudges**, **interlaced/progressive** switching, and **XInput gamepad** support; bitset publishes the full command matrix on the [command reference PDF](https://bitset.jp/download_file/view/323/323). Place **default.jpg** or **default.png** at the USB root for a **boot splash**.

Power from **Micro USB at 5 V / 2 A+**, including mobile batteries—bitset quotes **5+ hours** on a **10,000 mAh** pack as an example. Purchase via **Amazon.co.jp** or direct contact per [bitset.jp](https://bitset.jp/products/products-pondash-visual).
