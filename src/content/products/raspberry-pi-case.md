This kit pairs a **bitset-original Raspberry Pi enclosure** with a **3.5-inch LCD** path documented on [bitset.jp](https://bitset.jp/products/products-raspberry-pi-lcd-case): **nylon** parts in **white / black / blue**, **heat-tolerant** enough that the shell stays stable in **direct sun** (still protect the Pi and panel separately), and **vent slots** for convection—add a **USB fan** if needed.

It **stands upright** for bench use, and you can pick **open** vs **closed** **microSD** access depending on whether you favor swap speed or tamper resistance. The validated LCD is the **Kuman 3.5\" 480×320 SPI touch** module linked from bitset; **other modules are not guaranteed** to fit. Because SPI panels are **slow and low-res**, bitset recommends **framebuffer** workflows rather than expecting a comfortable **X11** desktop.

**Retail**: case-only via **DMM.make**, full kit via **Amazon.co.jp** (link above). Follow bitset’s **[assembly PDF](https://bitset.jp/download_file/view/246/325)**. **Schools and educators in Japan** can inquire about **evaluation loans** through the contact options on the legacy page.
